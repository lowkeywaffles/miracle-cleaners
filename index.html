Questions

Good to know

What is reweaving?

Reweaving repairs a hole, tear or burn by weaving new threads into the fabric so the damage nearly disappears. Bring the piece in and the owner will tell you what's possible.

Will you clean anything I bring?

We look over every item first. If something would do better hand-washed, we'll tell you honestly rather than risk your garment.

Are you open Saturdays?

Yes, Saturday mornings through late afternoon. See the hours above.
